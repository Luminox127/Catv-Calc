Humping
A signal build-up of the midband.
Causes: Over-equalizing amplifiers in the affected cascade, especially if EQ was used to correct roll-off.
Reflections (Standing Waves)
Stable symmetrical peaks/valleys across the band (sometimes only high end).

Cause: Impedance mismatch (not 75Ω). Common in amps with bi-directional testpoints, bad connectors, damaged faceplates, etc.
A quick distance check formula:

D = 492 (Vp / F)
D in feet, Vp = velocity of propagation (% of light), F = frequency width (MHz).
Roll-off
Frequency response level drop near upper or lower band edges.

Causes: loose connectors / seizure screws, loose modules, amplifier misalignment, diplex issues, bad splices (scraped center conductor), wrong band passives/EQ.
Notch
Sharp (often deep) negative dip.

Causes: loose connectors, tap/coupler faceplates, amplifier modules, internal RF grounding problems.
