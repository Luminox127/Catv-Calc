Reflections

Standing waves caused by impedance mismatch (not 75Ω). Common near amplifier testpoints and damaged connectors.

Humping

Midband buildup caused by over-equalization or incorrect cascade balancing.

Roll Off

High or low end level drop. Often from loose seizure screws, bad modules, diplex filter issues, or incorrect EQ.

Notch

Sharp negative dip. Usually loose connectors, tap faceplates, or grounding issues in amplifier.
